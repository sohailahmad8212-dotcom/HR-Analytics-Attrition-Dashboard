# 📊 HR Analytics & Attrition Risk Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition using SQL, Power BI, and Tableau to identify key workforce risk factors and support data-driven HR decision-making. The dashboard provides interactive visual insights into attrition trends, promotion risk, compensation impact, and employee satisfaction.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze employee attrition patterns across departments and job roles  
- Identify high-risk employees based on promotion delay and job satisfaction  
- Evaluate impact of salary, tenure, and overtime on attrition  
- Build interactive dashboards for workforce monitoring  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- SQL (MySQL) – Data cleaning and KPI calculation  
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard and KPI cards  
- Tableau Public – Data visualization and storytelling  
- Excel – Data preparation and preprocessing  

---

## 📂 Dataset
- Employee HR dataset containing workforce demographics, compensation, promotion history, and attrition status  
- Total Records: 50000 employees  
- Key fields:
  - Department  
  - Job Role  
  - Monthly Income  
  - Years Since Last Promotion  
  - Job Satisfaction  
  - Attrition  

---

## 📈 Key KPIs Developed
- Overall Attrition Rate (%)  
- Attrition by Department  
- Promotion Risk Level vs Attrition  
- Income Band vs Attrition  
- Job Satisfaction vs Attrition  
- Average Working Years  
- High-Risk Employee Segmentation  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Employees with promotion delays greater than 4 years have significantly higher attrition risk  
- Lower salary bands show increased turnover rates  
- Low job satisfaction strongly correlates with employee exits  
- Some departments experience higher attrition due to lower average tenure  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- Interactive KPI cards  
- Dynamic filtering by Department, Gender, Job Role  
- Promotion risk segmentation  
- Attrition trend analysis  
- Executive-level workforce summary
